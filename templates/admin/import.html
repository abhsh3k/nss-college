CSV, or Excel saved as .xlsx or .xls. Up to 8 MB and 500 students at a time. The first line is the header; the first sheet of a workbook is read.

Columns: admission_no, prn, name, email, phone. Required: admission_no and name. Optional: prn, email, phone, programme, semester, batch_year. Column names are matched loosely, so Admission No. or Permanent Registration No. works too.

Leave programme, semester and batch_year out of the file to put the whole list in the class chosen above. Any row that does carry them is placed in its own class instead, so one file can mix years and programmes.
